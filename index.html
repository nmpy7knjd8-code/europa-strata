    Languages ≠ genes

    Each region click shows family/situation, named languages when known, and an evidence tag (attested, reconstructed, hypothetical, or mixed). Yamnaya/Corded Ware expansions are often correlated with Indo-European dispersal — but pots, genomes, and speech can move separately (e.g. Iberian Beaker culture without automatic IE speech; CW/BB burial rites vs ancestry timing).

    Sources & honesty

    Deep ancestry components follow post-2015 genome-wide consensus (WHG, EEF/Anatolian Neolithic-related, Western Steppe Herders). Famous cases encoded include Beaker Britain (~90% Neolithic replacement), Denmark’s dual Holocene turnovers, and Iberia’s more gradual steppe admixture. Linguistic layer uses historical attestation where available and comparative reconstruction for deeper stages. Full bibliography: archaeogenetic-sources.md.

    • Haak et al. 2015 · Allentoft et al. 2015 — Steppe migration into Bronze Age Europe; Corded Ware as steppe-rich.
    • Olalde et al. 2018 — Beaker Britain ~90% turnover; continental Beaker genetic diversity.
    • Olalde et al. 2019 — Iberia: gradual, often male-biased steppe influx.
    • Allentoft et al. 2024 — Post-glacial western Eurasia “great divide”; Denmark turnovers ~60–85% steppe in Single Grave.
    • Antonio et al. 2019 — Imperial Rome eastern Mediterranean pulse; later rebalancing.
    • North Pontic genomic history (Nature 2024/25) — Yamna formation waves on the steppe.

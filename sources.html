Sources

Peer-reviewed genome-wide studies behind the atlas’s ancestry labels and percentages. Percentages in the atlas are rounded consensus ranges; models differ by method and sampling. Archaeological culture names are not genetic clusters, and languages are not genes. Every DOI below was checked against Crossref.
